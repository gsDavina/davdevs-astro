- I once met someone who'd taken "Apple" as their middle name—clearly a nod to the tech giant. Made me wonder what would happen if I met someone named "Orange" instead. At least nothing rhymes with it, so they'd never have to sit through a bad poem written for them. Turns out I have met one, and they only wear black—not for the phone brand, but because orange is the new black. Which makes them basically orange bits: small and citrus-flavoured, like an orange Tic Tac. So orange is the new breath, the new mint, or maybe just the new breadth—that last one's for Math to sort out. Then again, Apple owns a trillion-dollar empire while Orange is stuck running the farms—but at least Orange is feeding the masses.
