I’m excited to share my latest mini-project — **Dav/Devs Bible Proverbs** 📖✨  
A simple, quiet web app that automatically shows **the Proverb for today**.

This app is inspired by Pastor Joseph Prince’s encouragement:

> _“There are 31 chapters in Proverbs — one for each day of the month.”_

So instead of scrolling through apps or flipping pages, you simply open the site:

*   **1 Jan → Proverbs 1**
*   **29 Feb → Proverbs 29**
*   **31 Mar → Proverbs 31**

No clutter. No distractions. Just wisdom for the day.

> **Tech Stack**
> 
> *   **Framework:** Next.js
> *   **Language:** TypeScript
> *   **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (v4)
> *   **Content:** Public-domain Bible translations
> *   **Hosting:** Vercel
> *   **Focus:** Lightweight, readable, and intentionally simple

* * *

## 🎨 Themes & Reading Experience

I designed several preset themes for different preferences and contexts — from soft pinks and nature greens to newspaper-style reading and accessibility-friendly palettes.

Some highlights include:

*   **Dav/Devs Light / Dav/Devs Dark** – warm and familiar
*   **Simple Light / Simple Dark** – minimal and distraction-free
*   **Daily Herald** – classic newspaper feel
*   **Teh Tarik** – cozy browns inspired by Malaysia
*   **Vision Pro Light & Dark** – optimized for color-blind users
*   **Forest Grove** – nature-inspired greens
*   **Singapore Pride & Flag Patriot** – patriotic palette

Themes can be switched anytime, so readers can find the style they enjoy most.

* * *

## ⚙️ Customizable Settings

The app intentionally gives users control without complexity:

*   Text size options
*   Favourite default theme
*   Favourite translation
*   Configurable date format
*   Option to save preferences in the browser
*   Reset-to-defaults button

Everything is focused on **comfort, clarity, and ease of use**.
