A digital flashcard system for effective learning and memorization. Create, study, and track your progress with customizable cards featuring spaced repetition algorithms to enhance retention and accelerate learning.

### ✨ Features

*   **Custom Flashcards**: Create your own cards with questions and answers
*   **Spaced Repetition**: Smart algorithm shows cards when you need to review them
*   **Progress Tracking**: Monitor your learning progress over time
*   **Study Sessions**: Focused study modes for optimal learning
*   **Card Categories**: Organize cards by subject or topic

### � How to Use

1.  **Create Cards**: Click "Add New Card" and enter your question and answer
2.  **Organize by Topics**: Use categories or tags to group related cards together
3.  **Start Studying**: Begin a study session to review your flashcards
4.  **Rate Your Performance**: Mark each card as Easy, Good, or Hard based on your recall
5.  **Follow the Algorithm**: The spaced repetition system will schedule cards for optimal review timing
6.  **Track Progress**: Monitor your learning statistics and improvement over time
7.  **Regular Sessions**: Study consistently for best retention results
8.  **Edit Cards**: Update or refine cards as your understanding improves

**Study Tips**:

*   Keep card content concise and focused on single concepts
*   Review cards daily for maximum effectiveness
*   Be honest with your difficulty ratings to optimize the scheduling algorithm
*   Create cards for both directions (question-to-answer and answer-to-question)
