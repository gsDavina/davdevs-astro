Everyone's talking about AI. Almost nobody explains how to actually use it — without needing a computer science degree just to write a decent email.

> "Because using AI well is not random at all — it's just a really good prompt away."

## About

This book is for the everyday office worker who has heard all the buzz about AI and is thinking, "Okay… but how do I actually use this thing?" No computer science degree required — just a computer, a keyboard, and a willingness to get a little braver, smarter, and more playful with AI.
It's a friendly, jargon-free guide to making AI genuinely useful at work and beyond: honest comparisons of the major providers, prompting techniques that actually work, and everyday examples from budgeting to puppy care.
It's not random. It's just a really good prompt away.

## What's Inside

### 🤖 AI Provider Comparison

Honest field notes on Gemini, Copilot, Claude, and ChatGPT — what each one is actually good for, without the hype.

### ✍️ Prompt and Loop Engineering

Role prompting, few-shot prompting, prompt chaining, and chain-of-thought — explained without jargon, and how to keep refining until it lands.

### 🧰 AI for Everyday Life

Budgeting, itineraries, translations, jargon-busting, scheduling, and calculations — practical ways to bring AI into your day-to-day.

### 🎓 AI for Educators

Building exercises, checking student work, and differentiating for different levels — without a grading marathon.

### 🎯 Practice Playground

Nine hands-on exercises across everyday life, office work, and learning & growth — try them with whichever AI tool you have on hand.
