A friendly little guide to fixing bugs without losing your mind — for anyone who's ever stared at a red error message and panicked.

> “A gentle survival guide for anyone who has ever stared at a red error message and panicked.”

## About

Bugs don't mean you're bad at coding. They mean the computer got confused somewhere — and this book teaches you how to find out where. It walks you through reading error messages without flinching, reproducing and shrinking problems until they're easy to spot, and asking AI for help like someone who actually brought receipts.
Whether you're brand new to code or you've been quietly panicking at red text for months — this book is for you.
It's not scary. It's just a bug. And you can find it.

## What's Inside

### 🧯 First, Breathe. Then Read the Clues

How to read an error message without flinching — and actually understand what it's pointing at.

### 🕵️ You Don't Have to Debug Alone

AI, Google, and Stack Overflow as debugging buddies — and how to ask better than 'help, it broke.'

### 🧩 Make the Bug Show Up Again

Reproducing bugs on purpose, changing one thing at a time, and shrinking big problems into small ones.

### 🔍 Print Statements as Little Signboards

Checkpoints, tiny tests, and debuggers — simple ways to see exactly where things go off track.

### 📝 Be Kind to Future You

Useful logs, checking your assumptions, and version control as your coding time machine.

### 🎯 Practice Playground

Office-friendly exercises, from fixing a mystery expense total to untangling a 'sometimes broken' approval tracker.
