Get ready for a deliciously fun challenge! Catch healthy foods, avoid junk food, and collect amazing power-ups in this colorful emoji-based arcade game.

## How to Play

Move your basket 🧺 to catch falling food items as they rain down from the sky! But be strategic - not all food is created equal.

### Controls

*   **Mouse/Trackpad**: Move your cursor to control the basket
*   **Touch**: Tap and drag on mobile devices
*   **Keyboard**: Use arrow keys or A/D keys to move left and right
*   **Space Bar**: Pause/resume the game anytime

### Game Objective

**Score points** by catching healthy foods while **avoiding junk food** that will cost you lives. Survive as long as possible to achieve the highest score!

## 🎁 Power-Ups

Collect these special items for amazing temporary effects:

*   **⭐ Bonus Points** - Instant 50-point boost
*   **⏰ Slow Motion** - Slows falling speed for 5 seconds
*   **📏 Large Basket** - Doubles basket size for 5 seconds
*   **💖 Extra Life** - Adds one life (max 5 lives)
*   **🛡️ Safety Barrier** - Creates a bouncing barrier for 3 seconds

1.  **Prioritize healthy foods** - they're your main source of points
2.  **Collect power-ups** strategically for maximum benefit
3.  **Use the barrier power-up** when overwhelmed with falling items
4.  **Stay centered** to have better reach across the screen
5.  **Watch for patterns** in food spawning to optimize your positioning
6.  **Don't panic** - use the pause feature to take breaks
