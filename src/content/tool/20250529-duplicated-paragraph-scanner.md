The Duplicated Paragraph Scanner is a simple HTML and CSS tool that highlights repeated text blocks to improve clarity, reduce redundancy, and enhance writing flow—perfect for writers, editors, and students refining their drafts.

### ✨ Features

*   **Automatic Detection**: Instantly identifies duplicate paragraphs in your text
*   **Visual Highlighting**: Clear visual indicators for repeated content
*   **Clean Interface**: Simple paste-and-scan functionality
*   **Instant Results**: Real-time analysis as you work

### � How to Use

1.  **Paste Your Text**: Copy and paste your document or text content into the large text area
2.  **Scan for Duplicates**: Click the "Scan for Duplicates" button to analyze your text
3.  **Review Results**: Duplicated paragraphs will be highlighted with visual indicators
4.  **Identify Issues**: See exactly which paragraphs are repeated throughout your text
5.  **Edit Content**: Use the highlighting to guide your editing and remove redundant sections
6.  **Re-scan**: After making changes, scan again to ensure all duplicates are resolved

**Writing Tips**:

*   Focus on paragraphs that add new information or perspectives
*   Consider merging similar paragraphs rather than deleting them entirely
*   Use the scanner during your editing phase for best results

### �💡 Perfect For

Writers, editors, students, and content creators who want to ensure their text is free from redundancy and repetition.
